## Goals
- Formalize the research experiment package contract in specs.
- Add minimal validation to keep manifests/results/index aligned.
- Align public signals (spec statuses, repo naming, research clarity).
- Keep research outputs machine-generated and recordings external.
- Add an opt-in session-to-research packaging helper to reduce boilerplate.

## Constraints
- One change per commit.
- Spec-first: no changes to research tooling without approved spec.
- No recordings in repo; results generated by scripts only.
- Record a timestamp for each phase start and conclusion (ISO 8601 UTC).
